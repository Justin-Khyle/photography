    About

    Justin Khyle is a Filipino landscape and portrait photographer based in San Francisco.

    My interest in cameras began with my mom, who loved capturing life even before I was born, so it’s always been a constant in my life. My first love was actually videography. Like every kid in my generation, I made little vlogs on my phone, hoping to become some big YouTuber. Then came photography, a slow burn I never expected to fall in love with. Around 2022, I finally asked myself, why not actually try to get better?

    Since then, taking photos has become more than a hobby. It’s my escape. When I’m behind the camera, everything else fades away and I’m fully in the moment. Now I also get to capture special moments for other people and give them something they can cherish.

    My sessions are relaxed and easygoing, so you can just be yourself. You’ll have your photos within a week of the shoot.

    Graduating, celebrating something, or just want photos you'll actually like? Send me a message or email justinkhyle@gmail.com.

    FAQ

    What types of sessions do you offer?

    Portraits are my main focus. That includes graduation photos, individual portraits, and small groups like couples, friends and family. If you have something else in mind, just ask.

    How do I book, and what happens after that?
    1. Send me a message through the contact form or by email with what you have in mind and a few dates that work for you.
    2. I'll get back to you so we can plan the location, time and the kind of photos you want.
    3. We shoot at a relaxed pace, with no rushing.
    4. You get your edited photos within a week.
    I feel awkward in front of the camera. Will you help?

    Yes, and you're not alone. Most people feel a little stiff at first. I'll guide you with simple poses and direction throughout the shoot, and keep things easygoing so you can relax and just be yourself.
